UniTrack · University of Ibadan

Privacy notice

UniTrack stores the academic information you choose to provide so you can understand your progress.

What we collect

Account details, student profile information, courses, grades, credit units and academic preferences. We collect only what is needed to provide the service.

How we use it

We calculate GPA and CGPA, show graduation progress, surface deadlines and keep your account secure. We do not sell student data.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data or request account deletion through the authenticated privacy API. Contact the service operator before launch to add the official support address.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions are revocable, and access is restricted by account ownership and role. Production deployment must use HTTPS, managed secrets, backups and a designated data controller.
